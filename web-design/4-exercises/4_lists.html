How to Brew a Pour Over

  1. Boil water to 93°C
  2. Grind 20g of coffee to a medium-coarse setting
  3. Place filter in dripper and rinse with hot water
  4. Add coffee grounds and start timer
  5. Pour in three stages:
    • 0:00 — 50ml bloom pour
    • 0:45 — 150ml second pour
    • 1:30 — 150ml final pour
  6. Total brew time should be 3 minutes

Glossary

Extraction
The process of dissolving soluble compounds from coffee grounds into water.
Bloom
The initial small pour of water that releases CO2 from fresh coffee grounds.
TDS
Total Dissolved Solids — a measure of the strength and concentration of brewed coffee.
