Brew Guide

Follow our step-by-step guide to brew the perfect cup. Precision and timing are key to extracting the full flavor potential of our beans.

  1. Heat water to 195–205°F (90–96°C) using a gooseneck kettle for better control
  2. Grind your beans to a medium-coarse consistency, similar to sea salt
  3. Add ground coffee to your filter, using a 1:16 coffee-to-water ratio by weight
  4. Pour water slowly and deliberately:
    • First, bloom with 2x the weight of water for 30 seconds
    • Pour in circular motions, keeping the water level consistent
    • Complete pouring within 3–4 minutes total
    • Allow final drips to finish before removing the filter
  5. Wait 1–2 minutes for the coffee to cool slightly to optimal drinking temperature
  6. Pour into your favorite cup and savor the complex flavors

About

Roast & Co. was founded in 2015 with a simple mission: to bring exceptional specialty coffee to coffee lovers everywhere. We believe in direct relationships with farmers, sustainable practices, and meticulous roasting that honors each bean's unique character.

Every bag we roast is crafted with intention. We roast in small batches to ensure freshness, quality, and consistency. Our commitment to excellence extends from farm to cup.
