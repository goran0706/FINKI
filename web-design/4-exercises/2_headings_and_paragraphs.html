Coffee Brewing Methods

Pour Over

Pour over brewing involves slowly pouring hot water over ground coffee in a filter.

The result is a clean, bright cup that highlights the origin flavours of the bean.

French Press

French press uses immersion brewing, steeping coarse grounds directly in hot water.

After four minutes, the plunger is pressed down to separate the grounds from the liquid.

Espresso

Espresso forces pressurized hot water through finely ground, compacted coffee.

It forms the base of many coffee drinks including lattes, cappuccinos, and flat whites.
